# V1.5 Executive UX

## Scope

V1.5 Phase 1 upgrades only the owner dashboard. Project Detail, Finance Workspace and Procurement Workspace keep their existing V1.4 structure.

## Information Architecture

The dashboard now follows this decision order:

1. Executive Command Center: date, company scope, business state, current cash, 30-day outlook and the top three priorities.
2. Executive metrics: two primary cash metrics plus receivables, approvals, risky projects, missing invoices and active projects.
3. Cashflow Intelligence and Action Center.
4. On-demand AI Daily Brief.
5. Project Health and company fund structure.
6. Receivable aging and payable maturity.
7. Weekly business trend.
8. Recent business details at the bottom.

All values follow `Database -> Analytics -> UI`; AI does not produce chart data.

## Interaction

- KPI cards count up on entry, expose up to three contributing facts on hover, and link to the owning ledger.
- The cashflow area chart uses an animated gradient fill, crosshair tooltip, negative-balance marker and date drill-down.
- Project Health uses a horizontal score ranking. Hover explains budget, collection, procurement and risks; click opens the project ledger.
- Company Funds uses an animated donut with linked legend and center value. Selecting a company updates the owner company scope.
- Aging and maturity bars link to their filtered receivable or payable views.
- Action Center keeps a stable row height while hover reveals the risk reason and recommended human action.

## Motion And Responsive Behavior

Entry motion is 240 ms, chart motion is 720-780 ms, and hover motion is 150 ms. Hero ambient lines breathe over five seconds without large blur or continuous full-page repaint. `prefers-reduced-motion` disables meaningful animation.

At 375 px and other mobile sizes the page prioritizes business state, primary cash metrics, AI, actions and risk. Charts stack vertically, KPI hover overlays are disabled, and all controls remain within the viewport.
